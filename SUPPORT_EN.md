# SleepVoice Support

SleepVoice helps you record and review nighttime sound activity on your iPhone. The Apple Watch companion can show monitoring status and control a session on your paired iPhone.

## Getting started

1. Open SleepVoice and allow microphone access when prompted.
2. Before bed, tap **Start Monitoring** in the iPhone app, or start a session from your Apple Watch.
3. Stop monitoring when you wake up.
4. Browse events by date, play recordings, and export or delete recordings you no longer need.

If microphone access was denied, open **Settings > Apps > SleepVoice > Microphone** on your iPhone and turn it on, then return to SleepVoice.

## Privacy and storage

Recordings, sound events and classification results remain on your device. They are not uploaded to a developer server. See the [English privacy policy](PRIVACY_EN.md) for details.

## Contact support

For questions, bug reports or feedback, open an [issue in SleepVoice Support](https://github.com/niuwenyong/SleepVoice-Support/issues). Please do not attach private recordings unless you choose to share them.
