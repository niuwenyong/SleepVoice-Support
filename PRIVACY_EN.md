# SleepVoice Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 30, 2026**

SleepVoice respects your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Microphone and recordings

SleepVoice requests microphone permission to monitor nighttime sounds. It accesses the microphone for a monitoring session only after you start one and grant permission. Audio clips are used to record and classify sound activity during sleep.

Recordings, sound events and classification results are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive, collect or upload this content.

## Data collection and tracking

The developer does not collect personal data through SleepVoice. The app does not use advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or sell your data.

## Local storage and your choices

SleepVoice stores settings, sleep records, sound events and audio files locally. You can play, export or delete local recordings and records in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove this content. Exported files are handled by the destination you choose.

## Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion exchanges monitoring status and control commands with your paired iPhone to provide app functionality. This communication does not upload data to a developer server.

## Children's privacy

SleepVoice does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated text and effective date will be posted on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through [SleepVoice Support Issues](https://github.com/niuwenyong/SleepVoice-Support/issues).
